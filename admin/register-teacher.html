Use this page to create a teacher's sign-in account and EqualLearn profile. Confirm the teacher's identity and department using official school records before submitting.

Confirm the teacher's details Enter the official surname, first name, optional middle initial, and assigned department.
02
Prepare sign-in credentials Use a valid email address the teacher can access. It must not already be registered in EqualLearn.
03
Set a temporary password The password must contain 8 to 128 characters. Share it with the teacher through a secure channel.
What happens after creation?

EqualLearn creates a Firebase sign-in account and a Teacher profile with the assigned department, the Teacher role, and active status. The teacher can then sign in using the registered email and password.

Account creation is restricted to an authenticated administrator. The administrator's session remains active after the teacher account is created.

Teacher Information

Use the surname shown in the teacher's official school records.

Enter the teacher's official first or given name.

Optional. Enter the middle initial only, up to 5 characters.

Enter the department assigned by the school, for example GAD.

Account Credentials

Use an active email address the teacher can access. Gmail and institutional addresses are supported; an email already registered in EqualLearn cannot be reused.

Use 8 to 128 characters. Avoid reusing an administrator's or another person's password.

SECURITY Share the temporary password privately with the teacher. Do not send it in a public group or include it in an announcement. The teacher can use the Forgot Password feature if a password reset is needed.
